I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at [Texas A&M University](https://www.tamu.edu/), advised by Prof. [Yu Zhang](https://yuzhimanhua.github.io/), and a **Student Researcher at [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/)**. Previously, I was a visiting student at [Stanford University](https://www.stanford.edu/) working with Prof. [Yejin Choi](https://yejinc.github.io/) and Prof. [James Zou](https://www.james-zou.com/), an Applied Scientist Intern on the Amazon Rufus foundation model team, and a research intern at [TIGER-Lab](https://github.com/TIGER-AI-Lab) (University of Waterloo) with Prof. [Wenhu Chen](https://wenhuchen.github.io/).

My research is on **large-scale post-training and reinforcement learning for LLM agents**. Lately I focus on **recursive self-improvement (RSI)**: agents that get better by training on their own experience.

- **Agentic RL and training infrastructure.** [AgentFlow](https://agentflow.stanford.edu/) (ICLR 2026 Oral, 1.9K+ ⭐) and [VerlTool](https://github.com/TIGER-AI-Lab/verl-tool) (ICLR 2026 SPOT Best Paper, 950+ ⭐).
- **Agentic search and deep research.** [OpenResearcher](https://github.com/TIGER-AI-Lab/OpenResearcher) (adopted by [NVIDIA Nemotron](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/nemotron/projects/), 700+ ⭐) and [DCI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05242) (NeurIPS 2026 Spotlight, 400+ ⭐).
- **Recursive self-improvement.** Leading [OpenRSI-Index](https://github.com/OpenRSI-Foundation/OpenRSI-Index).
- **Evaluation of agents and models.** [ClawBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08523) (adopted by Meta Superintelligence Labs), [StructEval](https://tiger-ai-lab.github.io/StructEval/), [ImagenWorld](https://tiger-ai-lab.github.io/ImagenWorld/).
